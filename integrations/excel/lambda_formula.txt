# Microsoft Excel Zero-Install Formulas & LAMBDA Custom Function

## Option 1: Direct Formula (Zero Install, Works in any cell)
You can directly query the SEC API from any cell without installing any add-ins or macros:

Raw Number (for financial modeling and math):
=NUMBERVALUE(WEBSERVICE("http://localhost:8080/api/sec/" & A2 & "/" & B1 & "?raw=1"))

Formatted String (e.g. "$245.1B" or "34.2%"):
=WEBSERVICE("http://localhost:8080/api/sec/" & A2 & "/" & B1 & "?format=text")

Example for Microsoft Adjusted ROIC:
=NUMBERVALUE(WEBSERVICE("http://localhost:8080/api/sec/MSFT/ADJ_ROIC?raw=1"))

## Option 2: Define =SEC() in Excel Name Manager (Modern Excel 365 / 2021)
You can define a native =SEC(...) formula in Excel that feels just like a built-in Excel function:

1. In Excel, go to: Formulas tab > Name Manager (or Define Name).
2. Click "New".
3. Name: SEC
4. Refers to (paste this single formula):
=LAMBDA(ticker,metric,[period],NUMBERVALUE(WEBSERVICE("http://localhost:8080/api/sec/" & UPPER(TRIM(ticker)) & "/" & UPPER(TRIM(metric)) & "?period=" & IF(ISOMITTED(period),"TTM",period) & "&raw=1")))

5. Click OK.
6. Now you can type anywhere in your spreadsheet:
   =SEC("MSFT", "ADJ_ROIC")
   =SEC("AAPL", "REVENUE")
   =SEC("NVDA", "FCF", "TTM")
